What We Offer

Comprehensive
Dental Care

From preventive cleanings to advanced periodontal surgery and teeth whitening — all under one roof, with compassionate hands.

Dental Cleaning
Professional scaling, polishing, and preventive care to keep your teeth and gums healthy.
Dental Filling
Tooth-coloured composite fillings that restore function and blend naturally with your smile.
Dental X-rays
Digital X-ray imaging for accurate diagnosis and personalised treatment planning.
Dental Extraction
Safe, gentle tooth removal with proper anaesthesia and aftercare guidance.
Surgical Extraction
Complex extractions including impacted wisdom teeth, performed with precision and care.
Dentures
Custom-fitted full and partial dentures crafted for comfort, function, and a natural look.
Porcelain Crown & Bridge
High-quality dental porcelain crowns and bridges for strength and a natural appearance.
Root Canal Treatment
Save your natural tooth with expert endodontic treatment — more comfortable than you'd expect.
Teeth Whitening
Professional-grade whitening treatments that deliver dramatic results safely and quickly.
Periodontal Surgery
Advanced gum treatments to address periodontal disease and restore oral health.
